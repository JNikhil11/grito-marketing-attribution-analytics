# Marketing KPI Definitions & Mathematical Formulas

**Project:** Multi-Channel Marketing Analytics & Attribution Framework  
**Entity:** Grito Labs  
**Document Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Top-of-Funnel & Engagement Metrics

### 1.1 Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- **Definition:** The percentage of ad impressions that resulted in a user click.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{CTR} = \frac{\text{Total Clicks}}{\text{Total Impressions}} \times 100\%$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(CAST(clicks AS REAL) / NULLIF(impressions, 0) * 100.0, 2) AS ctr_pct
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** Measures creative relevance, thumbnail appeal, and targeting precision. Low CTR indicates audience mismatch or ad fatigue.

### 1.2 Cost Per Click (CPC)
- **Definition:** The average expenditure incurred for each user click directed to the landing page.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{CPC} = \frac{\text{Total Media Spend}}{\text{Total Clicks}}$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(spend / NULLIF(clicks, 0), 2) AS cpc
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** Measures auction bidding competitiveness and channel efficiency. Paid Search commands a higher CPC ($9.54) due to active commercial intent compared to Paid Social ($5.51) and Display ($1.93).

## 2. Conversion & Efficiency Metrics

### 2.1 Conversion Rate (CVR)
- **Definition:** The percentage of website sessions or clicks that culminate in a paid course enrollment.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{CVR} = \frac{\text{Total Conversions}}{\text{Total Clicks}} \times 100\%$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(CAST(conversions AS REAL) / NULLIF(clicks, 0) * 100.0, 2) AS cvr_pct
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** Reflects value proposition resonance, syllabus alignment, pricing clarity, and checkout friction.

### 2.2 Cost Per Acquisition / Customer Acquisition Cost (CPA / CAC)
- **Definition:** The total advertising capital required to acquire one newly enrolled student.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Media Spend}}{\text{Total Attributed Conversions}}$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(spend / NULLIF(attributed_conversions, 0), 2) AS cac
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** The primary benchmark for channel viability. Must be evaluated strictly against Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

## 3. Financial Return & Profitability Metrics

### 3.1 Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- **Definition:** Gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on paid advertising.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Attributed Revenue}}{\text{Total Media Spend}}$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(attributed_revenue / NULLIF(spend, 0), 2) AS roas
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** Gross multiplier on marketing spend. A ROAS of 18.4x means $18.40 in revenue per $1.00 ad spend.

### 3.2 Return on Investment (ROI)
- **Definition:** Net financial return generated by marketing expenditure expressed as a percentage.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Attributed Revenue} - \text{Total Media Spend}}{\text{Total Media Spend}} \times 100\%$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND((attributed_revenue - spend) / NULLIF(spend, 0) * 100.0, 2) AS roi_pct
  ```
- **Business Interpretation:** Indicates net value creation after deducting direct advertising costs.

## 4. Multi-Touch Journey & Funnel Role Metrics

### 4.1 Assist-to-Last-Click Ratio
- **Definition:** The ratio of intermediate assisting touchpoints to final closing touchpoints for a given marketing channel.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Assist-to-Last Ratio} = \frac{\sum \text{Assist Touches}}{\sum \text{Last Touches}}$$
- **SQL Implementation:**
  ```sql
  ROUND(CAST(SUM(is_assist) AS REAL) / NULLIF(SUM(is_last), 0), 2) AS assist_to_last_click_ratio
  ```
- **Strategic Thresholds:**
  - $> 1.5$: **Top/Mid-Funnel Feeder / Influencer** (Paid Social, Display Ads).
  - $0.8 - 1.5$: **Balanced Full-Funnel** (Organic Search, Referral).
  - $< 0.8$: **Bottom-Funnel Closer / Harvester** (Brand Search, Direct).

## 5. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) & Unit Economics

### 5.1 Historical CLV
- **Definition:** Cumulative revenue realized from a customer across all initial and follow-on repeat course purchases.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Historical CLV} = \frac{\sum \text{Lifetime Revenue}}{\text{Total Acquired Customers}}$$

### 5.2 Predictive 12-Month CLV (BG/NBD + Gamma-Gamma)
- **Definition:** Statistically forecasted 12-month future revenue per student accounting for transaction frequency, dropout rate, and average basket size:
  $$\text{Predictive CLV} = \text{Historical Revenue} + \left( E[Y(12) \mid x, t_x, T] \times E[M \mid x, \bar{x}] \right)$$

### 5.3 LTV:CAC Ratio
- **Definition:** The ratio of customer lifetime value to the initial cost incurred to acquire them.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{LTV:CAC} = \frac{\text{Historical (or Predictive) CLV}}{\text{Blended CAC}}$$
- **Executive Benchmarks:**
  - $< 1.0\text{x}$: **Value Destructive** (Losing money on every student).
  - $1.0\text{x} - 2.5\text{x}$: **Marginal / Breakeven** (High vulnerability to churn).
  - $3.0\text{x} - 5.0\text{x}$: **Industry Gold Standard** (Healthy, scalable SaaS/EdTech).
  - $> 5.0\text{x}$: **Under-Invested** (Massive headroom to aggressively scale spend).
